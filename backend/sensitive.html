SENSITIVE SKIN

Sensitive skin is a skin type that reacts easily to external factors such as skincare products, weather changes, pollution, and stress. People with sensitive skin often experience redness, itching, burning sensation, or irritation.

This skin type requires extra care and gentle products. Harsh chemicals, fragrances, and alcohol-based products should be avoided as they can damage the skin barrier and worsen sensitivity.

Using mild cleansers, soothing moisturizers, and sunscreen specially formulated for sensitive skin helps in maintaining skin health and reducing irritation over time.
