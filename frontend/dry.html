DRY SKIN

Dry skin occurs when the skin lacks sufficient moisture and natural oils. It often feels tight, rough, itchy, and may show flaky patches. Environmental factors such as cold weather, low humidity, and harsh soaps can worsen dryness.

Proper skincare for dry skin includes using gentle cleansers, applying rich moisturizers, and avoiding excessive washing. Hydration and sun protection also play an important role in maintaining skin health.
